      The rule this page applies

      • Profit or loss for a day is that day's balance minus the previous one (day 1: minus the starting balance).
      • Total profit is the last balance minus the starting balance.
      • The largest day is the biggest winning day. Losing days never count as the largest.
      • Consistent means total profit is above $0 and the largest day is at most the limit (30% by default) of total profit. Exactly at the limit passes.
      • Profit target required is the larger of the profit target and the largest day divided by the limit.
      • Pass means consistent and total profit at or above the target.
